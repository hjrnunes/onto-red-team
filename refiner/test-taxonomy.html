The enriched risk taxonomy maps policy concepts to AI safety risks from standards frameworks.

The taxonomy organizes risks into groups and entries:

  • Groups — broad risk categories (e.g. Content Safety, Privacy, Discrimination)
  • Entries — specific risks with cross-framework mappings showing how they relate to entries in other taxonomies
  • Mapping types — exact (identical concept), close (nearly identical), broad (more general), narrow (more specific), related (thematically connected)
  • Domain context summary — ontology axes and enumerations assigned to each risk for generating diverse test prompts
  • CURIE map — prefix-to-URI mappings for resolving cross-mapping identifiers (e.g. atlas:bias-001 → full URI)
